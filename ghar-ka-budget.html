📖 App Ke Baare Mein

Ghar Ka Budget aik mufat, asaan aur professional family budget management app hai jo Pakistan ke ghar ke liye khaas taur par banaya gaya hai. Is app ki madad se aap apne ghar ka maheena bhar ka budget plan kar sakte hain, ration track kar sakte hain aur rozana ke kharche manage kar sakte hain.

✨ Features

  • Monthly budget setup aur tracking
  • 150+ preloaded ration items
  • Automatic expense calculations
  • Bills management (Bijli, Gas, Paani, Internet)
  • Monthly reports aur charts
  • Dark mode support
  • Offline working — no internet needed
  • Local storage — data safe rahta hai

👩‍💻 Developer

Is app ko Hanana ne banaya hai. Hamara maqsad Pakistan ke ghar walon ko unke finances manage karne mein madad karna hai.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2025

Data Collection

Ghar Ka Budget app aapki koi bhi personal information collect nahi karta. Aapka sab data sirf aapki device ke local storage mein save hota hai.

Local Storage

Yeh app sirf browser ke localStorage use karta hai. Koi data kisi server par send nahi hota. Aap kabhi bhi apna data clear kar sakte hain.

Advertising

Is app mein Google AdSense ads show ho sakti hain. Google apni privacy policy ke mutabiq data use kar sakta hai. Aap Google ke ad settings mein jakar personalized ads off kar sakte hain.

Cookies

Yeh app khud koi cookies use nahi karta. AdSense apni cookies use kar sakti hai jo Google ki privacy policy ke under hain.
